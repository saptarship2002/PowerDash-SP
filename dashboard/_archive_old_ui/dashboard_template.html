Power Quality Voltage Variation & Harmonics

These two definitions differ by state: Gujarat measures "% of sample tests showing NIL deviation" (compliance rate); Maharashtra, Rajasthan measure "% of cases resolved within standard" (a different resolution-rate metric). Both read as higher-is-better percentages, so they're charted together with the distinction called out in each tooltip. Odisha reported voltage variation as a raw complaint count (0 complaints) rather than a percentage, so TPCODL/TPNODL are excluded from this chart — see Key Gaps.

Service Quality Transformer Restoration & Billing Complaints

"Transformer Failure" means two different things in this workbook. Everywhere except Gujarat it's a restoration-compliance rate against a 90–95% benchmark (higher = better). Gujarat's four DISCOMs instead reported a raw failure rate with no benchmark at all (lower = better) — charted separately below so the two are never conflated.
